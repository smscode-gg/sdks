# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming, and inclusive community for everyone, regardless of background or identity. Be respectful, assume good faith, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@smscode.gg**. All reports are reviewed confidentially.
